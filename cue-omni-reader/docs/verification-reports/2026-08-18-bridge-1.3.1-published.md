## What 1.3.1 fixes (per the release basis)

1.3.1 is a hotfix on the 1.3.0 surface with **no tool-surface change**. The legacy grant
response schema now accepts `omni.parse_grant.v2`:

- cube-mcp's v2 metering writer emits v2 grants for unprofiled local-file requests;
- the 1.3.0 Bridge rejected those with `CUBE_PROTOCOL_ERROR`, which the hotfix resolves.

Everything 1.3.0 documented remains true of 1.3.1: the five existing tool contracts are
unchanged, `read_outline` and URL-result local hydration are present, and `uninstall
--yes --json` removes a trusted 1.3.0 **or** 1.3.1 Bridge entry and restores a matching
trusted URL-only entry when available.
