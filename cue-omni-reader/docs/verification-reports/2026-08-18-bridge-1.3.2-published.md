## What 1.3.2 fixes (per the release basis)

1.3.2 is a hotfix on the 1.3.1 surface with **no tool-surface change**. cube-mcp's bare
`UNSUPPORTED_SOURCE` error response (missing the `status` discriminant field the Bridge's Zod
schema requires) was previously masked as a generic `REMOTE_PROTOCOL_ERROR`. The Bridge now
recognizes this exact shape — narrowed to `toolName === "parse"` and
`code === "UNSUPPORTED_SOURCE"` only, after three rounds of adversarial review each finding a
broader trigger unsafe — and surfaces it correctly instead of masking it.

Everything 1.3.1 documented remains true of 1.3.2: the six existing tool contracts are
unchanged, `read_outline` and URL-result local hydration are present, and the legacy grant
response schema still accepts `omni.parse_grant.v2`.
