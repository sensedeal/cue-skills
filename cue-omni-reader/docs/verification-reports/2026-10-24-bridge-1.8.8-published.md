## What changed

Bridge 1.8.8 keeps the frozen seven local tools and the frozen wire contracts. Completed remote URL results now deliver the same structured billing detail direct-upload results have had since 1.8.7.

- **Bounded optional read.** After a completed remote result with settled credits, the bridge calls the server's `get_billing_details` tool once. The whole request and a 16 KiB-capped body race a 2-second timer. The closed five-key envelope (`status`, `protocol_version`, `operation_id`, `billing`, `units`) must carry protocol `omni.billing_details.v1`, the exact operation id, and credits identical to the settled facts.
- **Absence is never failure.** An older server without the tool (JSON-RPC unknown-tool), HTTP errors, a timeout, a malformed envelope, an MCP `isError` result, or a credits mismatch resolves to absence: the completed content and the returned credits stand unchanged, and no units are invented.
- **Three completion paths.** The create, status, and result re-presentation paths — including a cancellation that loses the race to completion and a cleanup-pending recovery that observes a fresh completed status — pass their billing through the same enrichment before the durable bind.
- **Durable and monotonic.** Units bind into the local journal with the 1.8.7 rules: absent units may be filled once, later omissions preserve them, conflicts are rejected, and restart restores them with no remote refetch.
- **Server side (live).** Production cube-mcp `1.5.62` (tag cut from master `67e8e6a`, deploy-prod succeeded) serves `get_billing_details`: read-only, principal-scoped like `get_parse_status`, never billed, closed errors (`BILLING_DETAILS_NOT_RELEASED`/`NOT_FOUND`/`UNAVAILABLE`), no fabricated zeros.
