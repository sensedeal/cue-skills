## What changed

One agent-experience fix, no tool-shape change.

- **`INVALID_TOOL_ARGUMENTS` messages name the tool's expected fields.** A text-driven agent that guesses `operation_id` for an artifact tool (they take `result_id`) used to receive only "The Omni tool arguments are invalid." — no way to self-correct.
